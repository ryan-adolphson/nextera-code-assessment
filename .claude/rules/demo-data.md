# Demo data

- **Demo data:** `npm run db:seed` stays the fixed fixture (the CSVs: TURB001/TURB002, 2 days in Jan 2026) that tests rely on. `npm run db:seed:demo` is for demos: the fixture farms, 200 `DEMO_TURBINES` (TURB001–TURB200, 16–24 per farm: TURB001–TURB025 hand-placed as before, TURB026–TURB200 generated by `demoFleetTurbines()` from `DEMO_FARM_LAYOUT`, a contiguous id block per farm on a staggered grid within 3 km of the farm centre; ids ending in 7 uncommissioned), the default rules (gearbox > 100 °C error, power < 100 kW warn, pitch > 30° info; existing rules kept), 72 h of generated telemetry ending now with `telemetry_alerts`, and the test users. Idempotent: a re-run tops up to now (`SEED_NOW` ISO, `SEED_HOURS` 1–744 override). The seed generates 10 turbines at a time and inserts 5,000-reading transactions with `insertTelemetryBulk` (flagged readings via `insertTelemetryWithAlerts`, the rest via one `unnest` insert): 72 h ≈ 7 s, `demo:reset`'s 168 h (~403k readings) ≈ 17 s. Anomalies, relative to the seed's end: TURB001 frozen at 0 kW in strong wind for 6 h (72 readings, from −50 h to −44 h; ≈ 21 MWh lost), TURB002 pitch spike (−30 h) and gearbox stuck at 126.5 °C for 90 min (18 readings, from −6 h; power keeps changing), 20 stale turbines (10 %, on every farm, none with another anomaly): 6 stopped (`DEMO_ANOMALIES.stopped`: TURB013 −90 min, TURB053 −2 h, TURB091 −2.5 h, TURB139 −3 h, TURB160 −4 h, TURB195 −5 h; "No data in 60 min", red; the feed never publishes them), 14 lagging (`DEMO_ANOMALIES.lagging`: TURB011/030/047/063/102/117/152/186 20 min behind, "No data in 15 min", yellow; TURB018/036/070/085/124/173 45 min behind, "No data in 30 min", orange; the seed ends them at end − lag and each feed tick publishes their reading for the 5-min boundary at `floor(now) − lag` from `generateTelemetry`, so their pills stay put; `demoReportingLagMinutes` is the one rule for both), TURB008 gap (−20 h), late `received_at` on TURB005/014/021, and TURB999 claiming FARM01 (outside the fleet's TURB001–TURB200; the feed publishes it every tick, so it shows under Unallocated). **Unallocated entries** (`DEMO_UNALLOCATED`, `src/seed/demo-unallocated.ts`, held readings from `generateTelemetry` ending at the seed's end):
  - Pending:
    - TURB999→FARM01, unknown, 6 h;
    - TURB998→FARM99, unknown, farm doesn't exist, 3 h;
    - TURB003→FARM02, mismatch (it's on FARM01), 2 h;
    - TURB004→FARM99, mismatch, Move disabled, 1 h.
  - Blocked:
    - TURB997→FARM05, unknown, by admin@, end − 24 h;
    - TURB005→FARM03, mismatch, by owner@, end − 12 h.

  Written through the shared `classifyReadings`/`holdReadings`/`blockUnallocated`, like ingestion and the API. A re-run:
  - tops up pending entries;
  - never unblocks, and never blocks a pending entry;
  - skips a pair whose turbine is now on the reported farm (assigned or moved);
  - re-creates a kept or unblocked pair, as its next real reading would.

  A demo turbine's farm is set only when it's created, so a re-run doesn't undo a Move. The live feed (`apps/ingestion/src/demo-feed`) publishes one reading per demo turbine (194: not the 6 stopped ones; the lagging ones at their lag) plus TURB999 per tick (195 publishes) at the current 5-min boundary to `PUBSUB_TOPIC`, through Pub/Sub → ingestion like a real turbine (alerts, SSE). Env only: `DEMO_FEED_ENABLED` (must be `true`, else it logs "Demo feed disabled" and exits 0), `DEMO_FEED_INTERVAL_SECONDS` (300), `--once`/`DEMO_FEED_ONCE=true`; no DB, Redis or JWT. **Run `db:seed:demo` before enabling the feed:** the fixture has only TURB001/TURB002, readings for unknown turbines get 400, and the emulator (no DLQ) redelivers them forever (GCP: DLQ after `pubsub_max_delivery_attempts`). `npm run demo:reset` (`packages/shared/prisma/demo-reset.ts`) gives a fresh demo in one command: it refuses unless `DATABASE_URL` is localhost or the compose Postgres (`--allow-remote`) and asks for confirmation (`--yes`), deletes `telemetry_alerts`, `telemetry`, `held_telemetry`, `unallocated_turbines`, `farm_weather` and the farm rollup tables in one transaction (farms, turbines, users and rules stay, so a move stays), re-seeds 168 h up to now, syncs the weather (a failure is a warning), publishes one feed tick and (re)starts the compose `demo-feed` with `DEMO_FEED_ENABLED=true` as a shell override (rebuilding its image unless `--no-build`), or prints `DEMO_FEED_ENABLED=true npm run demo:feed` without the stack. `--no-weather` and `--no-feed` skip those steps.
